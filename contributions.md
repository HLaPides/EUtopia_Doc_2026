# Individual Contributions

### Sidra Ansari
Throughout the development of EUtopia, I contributed from early ideation and system design to implementation and final refinement. During the planning phase, I helped pivot the project toward the education sector and developed user personas and stories that guided the platform functionality. I played a major role in designing the database model, creating wireframes for the teacher and EU official interphase, and restructuring the DDL and REST API matrix to ensure a more organized and scalable architecture. I also managed much of the backend routing and worked to maintain consistent naming conventions and database integration across the whole project.

As development progressed, I implemented the initial routes that formed the basis of the Streamlit pages and expanded the functionality for all three user roles. For students, I improved the country simulation experience, added support for saving simulations, and refined assessments by introducing proper multiple-choice responses and open-ended questions. For teachers, I implemented access to both personal and global lessons, while for EU officials I developed the lesson approval system. A significant portion of the project involved troubleshooting and replacing hardcoded values with dynamic database-driven functionality, including user retrieval and country simulation values. These improvements increased the flexibility and maintainability of the platform and helped transform EUtopia into a cohesive educational application that integrates frontend design, backend development, database management, and machine learning features.
